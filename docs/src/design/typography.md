# Typography

Full detail: `DESIGN.md` §§10–14.

## Two Typefaces

| Typeface | Role | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Cormorant Garamond** | Emotion (display) | Hero headlines, manifesto, section headlines, brand statements |
| **DM Sans** | Function | Navigation, paragraphs, buttons, cards, metadata, forms, labels |

> Cormorant Garamond = Emotion. DM Sans = Function.

Do not introduce a third primary font, or reverse these roles, without explicit
project approval.

## Weights

- **Cormorant Garamond:** prefer Regular / Medium / SemiBold — avoid making every
  headline bold.
- **DM Sans:** Body → Regular, Navigation → Medium, Buttons → Medium/SemiBold,
  Labels → Medium, Important → SemiBold.

## Colour Rules

Headlines `#F5F1E8` · Paragraphs `#C9C3B8` · Metadata `#858078` · Accent `#C8A96B`
(sparingly — avoid large blocks of gold text).
