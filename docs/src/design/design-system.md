# Design System

The full, approved visual source of truth lives at **[`/DESIGN.md`](../../../DESIGN.md)**
in the repository root. This book does not duplicate it — the pages in this section are
short, scannable summaries with pointers into `DESIGN.md` by section number. When in
doubt, `DESIGN.md` wins.

## Visual North Star

> Build THE AVERAGE GUY like a warm, cinematic editorial magazine for a neighbourhood
> café — black canvas, champagne-gold details, warm-white typography, beautiful
> photography, generous negative space and subtle motion.

Premium without intimidating. Dark without cold. Golden without flashy. Minimal without
empty. *(DESIGN.md §§2–3)*

## Golden Rule

> Gold is a whisper, not a shout.

When in doubt: **choose restraint**. *(DESIGN.md §§29, 47)*

## What NOT to Build *(DESIGN.md §45)*

Generic restaurant templates, excessive gold, bright yellow gold, pure white
everywhere, excessive glassmorphism/gradients/rounded cards, SaaS dashboard
aesthetics, stock-photo look, excessive animation, random 3D, cursor effects, bouncy
UI, identical-looking sections.

## Design Quality Check *(DESIGN.md §46)*

Before considering a page complete, verify brand feel, colour balance, typography
roles, editorial layout, photography warmth, subtle motion, and intentional mobile
composition. See the full checklist in `DESIGN.md` §46 and cross-reference
[QA Checklist](../qa/checklist.md).
