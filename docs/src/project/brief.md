# Project Brief

**Project:** THE AVERAGE GUY — Café Website
**Type:** Premium interactive café website / digital community experience
**Core concept:** "We are building a community."

## Product Vision

The website presents THE AVERAGE GUY as a distinctive, community-focused café through
visual storytelling, menu information, event discovery, customer moments, upcoming
products, and clear visit/contact details. *(Source: SRS v1.0 §1.2)*

## Objectives and Success Criteria

- Make the café identity and manifesto immediately understandable.
- Help visitors discover menu items, events, products, and location details quickly.
- Encourage visits and social engagement through clear CTAs.
- Showcase authentic community content with moderation controls.
- Deliver a fast, responsive, accessible, and search-friendly experience.

Final measurable targets: **TBD** — to be agreed during project planning. *(SRS §2)*

## Stakeholders and Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Guest / Visitor | Browse pages, view menu, discover events, view community content, find location/contact details, access social channels. |
| Administrator / Content Manager | Manage and moderate content where CMS functionality is included. |
| Client Representative | Approve branding, content, imagery, requirements, and final acceptance. |
| Project Team | Design, develop, test, deploy, document, and hand over the website. |

*(Source: SRS v1.0 §3)*

## Excluded Features

Loyalty engagement, loyalty points, and priority notifications are explicitly excluded.
See [Scope](scope.md) for the full out-of-scope list.
